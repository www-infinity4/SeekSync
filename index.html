Thirteen classics, clipped and synced into one endless reel.

SeekSync stitches short cues from 13 public-domain movies and cartoons into a continuously looping, synchronized reel — pairing each clip with a companion score or track the way Astraflix pairs film and album. Add your own YouTube content through the Sync Console below and it joins the reel instantly. Every share of a clip accrues a provisional $0.01 Infinity Coin payout to that content's current rights owner in the shared ledger, and — if you connect your Unified Infinity Wallet — settles there too.
